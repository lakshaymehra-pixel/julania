About Us

India's Most Trusted
Digital Lending Partner

Julania Finance is an RBI-registered Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC) headquartered in Wazirpur, New Delhi. We provide collateral-free, 100% digital loans — Salary Advance, Personal, Business, and Emergency Loans — with same-day disbursal via IMPS/NEFT and complete transparency.

Our automated underwriting engine delivers real-time credit decisions in under 60 seconds. With minimal documentation (Aadhaar + PAN + salary slip), transparent APR, zero prepayment penalty, and strict adherence to RBI Fair Practices Code — we are India's most trusted digital NBFC for salaried professionals.

How It Works

How We Disburse Your Loan

Our end-to-end digital lending process ensures fast credit assessment and same-day disbursal — no queues, no paperwork.

01

Submit Loan Application

Complete a quick digital application with basic employment and income details — takes under 2 minutes. No branch visit, no paperwork.

02

Complete Digital KYC

Upload your Aadhaar, PAN & salary slip for instant e-KYC verification. No physical documents needed — 100% online.

03

Credit Assessment

Our automated underwriting system evaluates your creditworthiness and income eligibility instantly — real-time decision in 60 seconds.

04

Funds Disbursed

Sanctioned loan amount transferred directly to your registered bank account via IMPS/NEFT — same day, zero delay.
